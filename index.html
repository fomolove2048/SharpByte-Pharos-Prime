TermMax

Vault operator and Curator of Market A (USDC/APC). Manages the Range Order rate mechanism, lender fee, and performance fee on gross lender yield.

SharpByte

Curator of Market B (USDC/pALPHA) and overall vault curator. Administers the $PROS Merkl incentive campaign and step-wise reward disbursement.

Pharos Network

Host network for SharpByte Pharos Prime and underlying markets. Co-funds the incentive campaign via $PROS, matched by TermMax's $TMX allocation.
